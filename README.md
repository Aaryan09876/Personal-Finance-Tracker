# Personal-Finance-Tracker

Personal Finance Tracker is a Python application that helps you monitor income, expenses, and savings from the command line or web UI. It lets you log transactions, categorize spending, set monthly budgets, and visualize your financial habits over time. The goal is to give you clear insights into where your money goes without needing spreadsheets or paid apps. Data is stored locally in CSV or SQLite so you own your financial records.

#FEATURES

1.Transaction Logging*: Add income and expenses with date, amount, category, and notes

2.Budget Management*: Set monthly limits per category and track spending vs budget

3.Data Visualization*: Generate bar charts and pie charts of spending by category using matplotlib

4.Reports*: Export monthly/annual summaries to CSV or PDF for taxes and review

5.Search & Filter*: Find transactions by date range, category, or keyword

6.Local Storage*: All data stays on your machine in CSV or SQLite - no cloud, no tracking
